Input Tax Capture

Capture GST bills from your phone and receive an accurate Input Tax Statement, ready for filing.

  • Take a photo of each bill or upload it from your phone or computer.
  • The supplier, invoice number and GST amounts are read into a table you can check and correct.
  • Your bills are added up into an Input Tax Statement for MIRA.
  • Sign in with your mobile number and pay only for the credits you use.

Photo Bot

Turn product photos into ready-to-post posters: remove the background, add your frame and the name in English or Dhivehi.

  • Backgrounds are removed automatically, for one photo or many at once.
  • 18 frames in three sizes: 4:5, square and story.
  • Product names in English and Dhivehi, with a choice of Dhivehi fonts.
  • Your logo, phone number, Instagram and website on every poster, plus offer stickers.
  • Save to your phone or send straight to Telegram. One credit per poster.
